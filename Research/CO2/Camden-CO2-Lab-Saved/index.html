Reference, baseline and PALM-CO₂

The reference case is 16 June 2019 at 19:30 UTC, for one 30-minute prediction step. The baseline is the surrogate prediction with unchanged reference inputs: 100% emissions, 100% wind speed, 0° rotation and 0 ppm boundary shift. Your scenario changes those inputs; change means scenario minus baseline.

PALM-CO₂ is the physics-based simulation used for comparison. It is separate from the surrogate baseline. Compare the baseline with PALM-CO₂ for the original reference case. Changed scenarios have no matching PALM validation in this app.

Li, Zheng & Fang (2026), A high-resolution urban CO₂ transport model with anthropogenic and biogenic fluxes, Geoscientific Model Development, 19 (14), 6417–6449.

About this demonstration

Every selectable input combination loads a prediction generated beforehand with the Camden V12 weather and V17 CO₂ models over the full domain. Tiles display crops of those same whole-domain fields. All 16 heights are available. The maps show atmospheric cells inside Camden. The saved fields use compact storage with a maximum checked concentration error of 0.001 ppm. The PALM comparison covers the original reference inputs.

Atmospheric inputs. Meteorological forcing comes from ERA5; background CO₂ comes from CAMS EGG4. Anthropogenic emissions are derived from the London Atmospheric Emissions Inventory (LAEI), disaggregated to 10 m; PALM-CO₂ calculates biogenic fluxes using VPRM. See Li, Zheng & Fang (2026), A high-resolution urban CO₂ transport model with anthropogenic and biogenic fluxes, Geoscientific Model Development, 19 (14), 6417–6449.

Geographic outline. The Camden outline is a display and analysis mask, separate from the atmospheric boundary conditions. ERA5 supplies meteorology, not the borough outline.
